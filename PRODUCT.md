# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in India who may be experiencing a cyber incident, including users who are stressed, uncertain which category applies, or need an immediate safety or recovery action before completing a report.

## Product Purpose

Cyber First Response helps people understand what to do after a cyber incident, choose a reporting route in plain language, preserve useful evidence, submit a report, track a complaint, check suspicious identifiers, and learn prevention guidance.

## Positioning

The service starts with what the person experienced instead of requiring them to know an official cybercrime category, then connects that account to immediate actions and the appropriate reporting flow.

## Operating Context

The primary journey may begin immediately after financial loss, threats, harassment, impersonation, account compromise, device or data attack, suspicious contact, or an incident involving a child. Users may be on mobile devices, shared devices, or unreliable connections and may return later to track progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js application built for OpenAI Sites.
- Uses `ux4g-web-components@2.0.1` and the UX4G design system as the component and token authority.
- Supports reporting, complaint tracking, suspicious-identifier checks, help and safety guidance.
- The public-facing interface must read as a real service. Do not display prototype, simulated-data, mock-data, or hackathon disclaimers.
- Do not present the homepage as primarily a financial-fraud service; all major cyber-incident types need equal, understandable entry points.
- Emergency and financial-loss actions must remain prominent without overwhelming every other reporting route.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Cyber First Response.
- Voice: direct, calm, plain-language, non-judgmental and action-oriented.
- Visual references supplied by the user establish an official civic-service character: strong navy hierarchy, white and light-neutral surfaces, crisp dividers, restrained orange/red urgency accents, compact utility navigation, and information-dense but legible service panels.

## Product Principles

- Put immediate safety before form completion.
- Let people describe experiences in everyday language.
- Keep all cybercrime categories visible and balanced.
- Make each next action explicit and reversible.
- Earn trust through clear structure, privacy explanations and honest claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible focus states, keyboard-operable controls, semantic headings and landmarks, labelled inputs, accessible error feedback, minimum 44px interactive targets, responsive layouts without horizontal scrolling, and reduced-motion support.
